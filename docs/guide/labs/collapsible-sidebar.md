# Collapsible sidebar

**What it does.** Adds a Close sidebar button beside the logo. It folds the sidebar into a narrow column of icons, so the pane on the right gets more room. Each icon shows its name when you point at it.

**Turn it on when.** The window is small, or you'd rather give the space to History, Output, or Meetings.

**Leave it off when.** Nothing to lose: the sidebar stays as wide as before.

**Needs.** Nothing else.

**Good to know.** In the narrow column the logo turns into Open sidebar when you point at it, and the panes, Labs, and About are icons. The update button stays at the bottom. VibeHex remembers whether the sidebar was folded. Turning the switch off shows the full sidebar again.

**Where.** Labs › Collapsible sidebar (`labs.collapsible_sidebar`).

**Asked as.** "Make the sidebar narrower"; "Hide the left panel"; «Как свернуть боковую панель»; «Хочу больше места под окно History».
