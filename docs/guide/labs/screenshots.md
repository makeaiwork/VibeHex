# Screenshots

**What it does.** Press ⌘⇧4 and drag an area, press Space for a window, or click for the whole screen. The shot is copied, saved (the Desktop by default), and shown as a corner card; click it to mark it up or talk over it.

**Turn it on when.** You want screenshots with marks and spoken notes.

**Leave it off when.** macOS's screenshots are enough.

**Needs.** macOS and Screen Recording permission. Turn off macOS's own ⌘⇧4 in Keyboard Shortcuts › Screenshots, or pick another shortcut. Record screen needs Annotate on.

**Good to know.** A Captures pane under History lists the last 30 days.

**Where.** Labs › Screenshots (`labs.screenshots`). Related: Shortcut (`screenshots.hotkey`), Record screen (`screenshots.record_hotkey`), Saves to (`screenshots.folder`).
