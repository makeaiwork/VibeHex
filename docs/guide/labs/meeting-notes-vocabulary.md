# Vocabulary in meeting notes

**What it does.** The model writing meeting titles and summaries may misspell names. With this on, it reads your Vocabulary first, so names come out as you wrote them.

**Turn it on when.** Your meetings mention names or jargon from your Vocabulary.

**Leave it off when.** Your Vocabulary is empty.

**Needs.** Meetings, with something set up to write notes. Terms in General › Vocabulary.

**Good to know.** Only the first 30 terms count. It applies to final titles and summaries, not to titles shown while recording. Russian vibe coding turns it on too.

**Where.** Labs › Vocabulary in meeting notes (`labs.meeting_notes_vocabulary`).

**Asked as.** "Names are misspelled in meeting summaries"; «В саммари встречи неправильно пишутся имена».
