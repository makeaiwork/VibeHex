# Notch indicator

**What it does.** On a MacBook screen with a notch, the recording indicator grows sideways out of the notch instead of sitting below the menu bar.

**Turn it on when.** You dictate on a notched MacBook.

**Leave it off when.** You prefer the usual indicator.

**Needs.** macOS and a screen with a notch.

**Good to know.** Works at the Top or Bottom position; Hidden still hides it. Flat external displays keep the usual indicator. Applies from the next dictation.

**Where.** Labs › Notch indicator (`labs.notch_indicator`).

**Asked as.** "Can the indicator use the notch?"; «Индикатор записи в вырезе экрана».
