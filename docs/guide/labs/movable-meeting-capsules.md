# Movable meeting capsules

**What it does.** VibeHex shows small floating capsules around meetings: an
offer to record, a reminder, "left the call", Meeting transcript ready, and
Recording automatically. They sit in the top right and can cover another app's
buttons. With this on, you drag a capsule to any screen corner. It snaps to
the nearest corner, opens there next time, and the recording capsule uses
that corner too.

**Turn it on when.** A capsule covers a toolbar, a call app's controls, or
window buttons.

**Leave it off when.** The top right works for you. Off, these capsules open at
the top right below the menu bar and don't drag; the recording capsule
drags either way.

**Needs.** macOS and Meetings turned on.

**Good to know.** Pressing a button inside a capsule still presses it. Escape
while dragging puts the capsule back without saving.

**Where.** Labs › Movable meeting capsules (`labs.movable_meeting_capsules`).

**Asked as.** "Move the recording capsule"; "The meeting popup covers my
buttons"; «Как перетащить плашку записи в другой угол»; «Окошко встречи
закрывает кнопки».
