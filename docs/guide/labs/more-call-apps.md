# More call apps

**What it does.** VibeHex offers to record calls it recognizes. With this on, it also recognizes Signal, Telegram, WeChat, LINE, Webex, Element, and the Phone app, and on a Mac names calls on Google Voice, Messenger, Teams, FaceTime, GroupMe, and Webex pages.

**Turn it on when.** You take calls there and want recording offered.

**Leave it off when.** You don't want offers for them.

**Needs.** Meetings turned on.

**Good to know.** Messaging apps wait about 20 seconds before offering, since voice notes use the microphone too.

**Where.** Labs › More call apps (`labs.more_call_apps`).

**Asked as.** "Record Telegram calls"; «Записывать звонки в Телеграме».
