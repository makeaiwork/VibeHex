# Settings search

**What it does.** Puts a Search settings field at the top of General. Type a word and only the rows whose title or description match stay.

**Turn it on when.** You can't remember which section a setting is in.

**Leave it off when.** Nothing to lose; off, General is as before.

**Needs.** Nothing else.

**Good to know.** Rows under Advanced match without opening it. Permission and error cards always stay. It searches General only. On a Mac, ⌘F in General jumps to the field.

**Where.** Labs › Settings search (`labs.settings_search`).

**Asked as.** "How do I find a setting?"; «Поиск по настройкам».
