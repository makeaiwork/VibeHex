# Dictation stats

**What it does.** Adds a "Dictation stats" block at the top of History: how many dictations you kept, how many words, words per minute, how often Output changed the text, your daily streak, and the five apps you dictate into most.

**Turn it on when.** You want to see how much you dictate, or how fast you speak.

**Leave it off when.** Nothing to lose: History looks exactly as before without it.

**Needs.** History turned on, with some dictations kept. The numbers count only what History still holds, so they shrink as old entries expire under your retention choice.

**Good to know.** The same summary is printed by the `hex history stats` command in a terminal, which works only while this switch is on.

**Where.** Labs › Dictation stats (`labs.history_stats`). Related: the History pane.

**Asked as.** "How many words have I dictated?"; "Where is my dictation statistics?"; «Где посмотреть статистику диктовок»; «Сколько слов я надиктовал».
