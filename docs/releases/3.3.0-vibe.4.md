# VibeHex 3.3.0-vibe.4

- New: When an update is ready, a window lists what changed in it and in every release you
  skipped, with Restart to update and Not now.
- New in Labs: Add to vocabulary from a selection (macOS). Select a misheard word in any app
  and press a shortcut, Option+Shift+A or a modifier alone such as Right Control.
- New in Labs: Cleanup progress (macOS). A long cleanup shows a bar in place of the HUD, with
  Paste now to paste what is cleaned so far.
- Changed: AI cleanup works on a dictation of any length; a long one is cleaned in parts.
- Changed: Paste vocabulary as a list is now Vocabulary text view: the whole list as text,
  one term per line, to paste, fix, or delete many terms at once.
- Changed: Labs shows its features in titled sections, has a search field, and stars the
  features VibeHex added.
- Changed: Built-in cleanup models load when you press Use and show their progress; Delete
  asks first.
- Fixed: AI cleanup writes your Vocabulary's terms more often instead of the misheard spelling.
