# VibeHex 3.3.0-vibe.1

The first VibeHex: Hex 3.3.0 as a Mac app of its own, with features Hex doesn't have.

- New: VibeHex is its own app and updates itself from its releases on GitHub. It shares
  settings, History, Vocabulary, and models with Hex; macOS asks for its permissions again.
- New in Labs: Clean up on your own server. AI cleanup runs on OpenRouter or a server of
  your own, such as LM Studio or Ollama, not only on OpenCode; a list button shows its models.
- New in Labs: Russian vibe coding. One switch adds 80 tech terms, word fixes, and Russian
  cleanup instructions, for Russian speech full of English words («клод код» to Claude Code).
- New in Labs: Paste vocabulary as a list, to add many terms to Vocabulary at once.
- New: Meetings recognizes Yandex Telemost calls.
- Fixed: Paste, Copy, and Select All work in VibeHex's fields with a Russian keyboard layout.
- Fixed: A long pasted list or instruction scrolls inside its field instead of pushing the
  buttons off the window.
